    What this is, and what it isn't

    A student prototype, not affiliated with the Nobel Foundation. Relations come from Wikidata and are shown with the provenance they actually have: some claims carry references and some carry none, and the difference is printed rather than smoothed over.

    Richard Tol's study The Nobel family (2024) reports that 696 of 727 laureates in these four categories sit in a single academic family tree. That is his dataset, not this one. Every number on this page is counted from the file this page ships with, and the two are not the same graph.
