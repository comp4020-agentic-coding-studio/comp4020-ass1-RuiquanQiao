They are Wikidata P18 images resolved through Wikimedia Commons, fetched on 2026-08-16 at 96px and committed to this repository, because the build is not allowed to touch the network and a page that loaded them live would be betting on somebody else's uptime.

The 19 laureates missing from this list have no picture on Wikidata at all.
